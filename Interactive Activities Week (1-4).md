These topics helped develop an understanding of how encryption, certificates, and secure communication are used to protect data over networks.

# Overall Reflection
The Week 1–4 activities helped me develop a stronger understanding of network security concepts and practical security techniques. These activities also improved my understanding of how different security controls work together to protect systems, users, and network communications.
